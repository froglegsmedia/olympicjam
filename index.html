The road to champion

  1. Scouting rounds

    Anyone can send in a jam. Our scouts listen to every one.

  2. Qualifications

    Scouted jammers go again to earn a spot in the tournament.

  3. Tournament

    The bracket starts. Win your jam and move up.

  4. Champion

    One jammer takes the title.

What wins

This isn't a shred-off. Judges listen for your jamming voice: the sound that's yours, the feel you bring and how you lock in with the moment. Technical skill still counts. It's just not the whole score.
